Our Mission

Our mission is to expand interest and understanding of tanks and other infantry fighting vehicles (IFVs). We aim to provide some of the most accurate, accessible, and engaging information available. Whether you're a long-time enthusiast or just starting to explore, our goal is to make learning about these vehicles both fun and reliable.

Our Story

I'm creating this website both as a way to share my passion and as a tool to help others learn. It's a personal project driven by my genuine interest in the subject, but I also hope it can serve as a valuable educational resource. By organizing information in a clear and accessible way, I want to make it easier for others to explore, understand, and enjoy these topics. Whether someone is just starting to learn or already has some knowledge, my goal is to offer a space where curiosity is encouraged and learning feels exciting.

Meet the Team

Our team? Just one guy — Rob Combs. He's not exactly a world-renowned tank expert (yet), but he does know his way around a good tank fact or two. Armed with a keyboard, a stubborn love for history, and way too much coffee, Rob is on a mission to dive deep into the world of armored vehicles and share everything he finds. Sure, he might not have a Ph.D. in Tanks (sadly, that's not a real thing), but what he lacks in formal titles, he makes up for with enthusiasm, curiosity, and an unhealthy number of bookmarked articles. If there's a weird story or obscure fact about a tank, odds are Rob's already tracked it down — and he's excited to bring you along for the ride. (FYI hes a bit of a funny guy if you couldn't tell.)
